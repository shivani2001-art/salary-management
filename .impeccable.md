## Design Context

### Users
ACME's HR Manager, on a desktop or laptop during the workday, looking after salary records for ~10,000 employees
across 10 countries. Today they work in Excel. They come to the app to (1) find and correct a person's salary
quickly and (2) answer "how do we pay people?" questions such as headcount, payroll, and what a role earns
in a country, often to share with leadership.

### Brand Personality
**Bold, modern, confident.** Numbers are stated plainly and large, as answers rather than raw data. Emotional goal:
the HR Manager feels in command of the data and proud to screen-share it. Copy is plain, sentence case and
active ("Add employee", "Save changes").

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light-first and designed for office daylight. Dark mode follows the OS and is a full theme, not an inversion.
- Palette: deep **pine green** (money, steady) as the primary, a **chartreuse highlighter** as the single loud
  accent (used as a marker swipe behind key numbers, never as text color), and ink and paper neutrals tinted toward pine.
- Type: **Bricolage Grotesque** for display (headlines, big numbers), **Instrument Sans** for UI and body, with
  tabular figures for all money.
- Signature: **pay bands**. Every breakdown row draws its min–max salary range on a shared scale, with a median tick
  and an average dot, the way compensation teams actually reason about pay.
- Material: **glass**, chosen by the owner. Frosted, translucent surfaces (app bar, controls, tables, panel, cards)
  float over a slow ambient light field in pine, chartreuse and lagoon. The field gives the blur something to show; a
  surface that sits over nothing stays plain. Blur falls back to near-opaque surfaces, and `prefers-reduced-transparency`
  turns it off.
- Anti-references: generic SaaS dashboards (four identical metric cards, purple gradients), cream-and-serif editorial,
  neon-on-black.

### Design Principles
1. **Answer, don't display.** Lead with a sentence that states the answer ("ACME pays 1,926 people in India $57.1M a year").
2. **One loud thing per screen.** The highlighter and the pay bands carry the boldness; everything else is quiet.
3. **Money is typography.** Use tabular figures, right alignment and consistent formatting everywhere.
4. **Stay in context.** Edit in a side drawer next to the list, confirm deletes inline, and don't use blocking modals for routine work.
5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, labelled controls, and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
